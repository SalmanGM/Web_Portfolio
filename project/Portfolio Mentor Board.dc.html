1Review of v6 + five ideas to riff on
What's working A place-based identity: Riyadh, At-Turaif and Arabic/English. Few portfolios have one. Tactile craft: paper, ink, film and tape all read as one material system. The work is organised by role (Strategy, Research, Visual), which matches how hiring managers scan.
What's costing you offers No real work visible yet. Every screenshot, painting and the portrait are empty slots. Fixing this matters more than any effect. No proof above the fold. Reviewers decide in about a minute. Outcomes (numbers, shipped scale) have to show before the decoration. Weight. Preloader, filters and an ~200KB file. Two console errors still need tracing, and mobile hasn't been checked.
Roadmap, in order 1. Real assets in: screenshots, portrait, three paintings. 2. Proof layer: one outcome line per project, on the card and in the hero. 3. One signature interaction people remember (pick from below). 4. Case study template: problem, move, result, with before/after. 5. Ship pass: mobile, speed, errors, share image, domain, analytics.
